## 1. Executive Summary

Synchrotech 2K26 is presented as the **flagship intra-university fest of the Department of Computational Studies**, bringing together first- and second-year students through technical and non-technical competitions.

The brochure positions the fest around three ideas:

- **Competition** — participants compete across specialized domains and events.
- **Learning and collaboration** — the events are designed around problem-solving, teamwork, communication, creativity, and technical skills.
- **Recognition** — performance accumulates toward individual domain-level recognition (**Stars of Synchrotech**) and a domain-level **Overall Champions** award.

The brochure establishes:

- **7 domains**
- **12 unique events**
- **26 scheduled event sessions/occurrences across the five-day schedule**
- A five-day event period from **7–11 September 2026**
- Domain-specific registration through QR codes
- Domain heads/contact persons for each domain
- A final-day championship/recognition structure
- A strong visual identity based on **deep space, galaxies, planets, nebulae, neon lighting, futuristic interfaces, and domain-specific color coding**

The brochure is not simply informational; it is designed as a **high-impact sci-fi event identity system**. The visual language changes by domain while maintaining a common cosmic/futuristic foundation.

# 5. Stars of Synchrotech

## Page 3

**Stars of Synchrotech** is the individual recognition system.

### Eligibility / concept

An individual who secures the highest cumulative points by participating in **all domain-specific events** becomes the Star of that domain.

The brochure states that each domain's top performer receives a unique title of honor.

### Total

The brochure explicitly states:

**7 Stars of Synchrotech**

The brochure does **not** provide the names of the seven titles. It only states that each domain's top performer will carry a unique title of honor.

# 7. General Guidelines

## Page 5

The brochure specifies the following rules.

### Registration

- Each participant must register under their respective domain only.
- Each domain can nominate a maximum of **2 participants for Spectrum CEO**.
- To be eligible for Spectrum CEO, a participant must have participated in **all domain-specific events**.

### Cross-domain participation

A participant who wishes to participate in another domain should have participated in all events belonging to their own domain.

### Conduct

- Malpractice, misconduct, or violation of event rules leads to disqualification.
- Judges' and coordinators' decisions are final and binding.
- Participants must maintain discipline and follow coordinator instructions.

### Dress code

A formal and proper dress code must be followed throughout the fest.

### Identification

All participants must carry a valid college/institution ID card throughout the event.

### Recognition date

The titles **Stars of Synchrotech** and **Overall Champions** are awarded on the finale day:

**11 September 2026**

## 54.7 Day 5

# 59. Potential Design Improvements

If a revised version is ever produced:

## 1. Standardize domain count

Resolve the **7 vs 8 domain** discrepancy.

## 2. Standardize names

Choose one official spelling for:

- AI/ML vs AIML
- Blockchain vs Block Chain
- DataForge vs Data Forge

## 3. Increase event-rule detail

The event descriptions explain the concepts well but often omit:

- Duration of individual rounds
- Team size
- Maximum participants
- Scoring
- Elimination rules
- Tools/software required
- Equipment requirements
- Winner criteria

These could be added in a dedicated rules appendix.

## 4. Explain repeated schedule sessions

The repeated events across multiple days need a short explanatory note.

## 5. Improve schedule readability

The table pages are visually attractive but compact. A revised version could increase:

- Table font size
- Venue readability
- Date grouping
- Domain color coding

## 6. Add a clear master registration/index page

The brochure currently distributes registration QR codes across domain pages. A consolidated registration page would improve usability.
